This section contains all legal documentation and privacy policies for **AI Playground**, an on-device generative AI studio engineered by Dhruv Patel ([@DearDhruv](https://github.com/DearDhruv)).

AI Playground is engineered with an uncompromising **offline-first and privacy-by-design architecture**. All neural network inference (LLMs, document processing, and diffusion models) executes locally on your device's NPU, GPU, and CPU hardware via LiteRT and MediaPipe without transmitting your prompts, inputs, or outputs to remote cloud servers.
