This section contains the legal documentation, acceptable use rules, and privacy disclosures for **AI Playground** (`com.deardhruv.llmplayground`), an on-device generative AI application developed by Dhruv Patel ([@DearDhruv](https://github.com/DearDhruv)).

AI Playground runs AI model inference locally on your device's CPU, GPU, and NPU hardware via `llama.cpp`, Google AI Edge LiteRT-LM, MediaPipe, `stable-diffusion.cpp`, and Apple MLX. Your prompts, chat history, documents, and generated images stay on your device. Optional user-initiated network features (such as Hugging Face Hub model search/downloads, optional live web search, and optional Google Play GPU engine delivery) and optional opt-in Firebase telemetry (off by default) are disclosed in our Privacy Policy.
