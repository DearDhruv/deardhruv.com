Welcome to the central legal, privacy, and compliance directory for all software products, mobile applications, games, developer utilities, and web services authored and maintained by **Dhruv Patel** ([@DearDhruv](https://github.com/DearDhruv)).

As an engineer specializing in on-device AI and high-performance mobile systems, my primary design philosophy is **local-first, privacy-by-design, and zero unnecessary telemetry**. Wherever feasible, data processed by applications resides exclusively in local hardware memory, and network communications are transparent, encrypted, and strictly limited to explicit user actions.

## Website Privacy & Digital Hygiene (`deardhruv.com`)

The personal engineering notebook and portfolio hosted at `deardhruv.com` is committed to minimal digital footprint:

* **No Tracking Cookies:** This website does not set tracking cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or cross-site fingerprinting identifiers.
* **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not employ Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any third-party marketing trackers.
* **Static & Encrypted Delivery:** The site is delivered as pre-rendered static HTML over TLS (HTTPS) through secure global edge nodes.
* **Theme Preferences:** Theme (`dark`/`light`) and color palette selections are persisted strictly in your browser's local storage (`localStorage`) and never leave your device.
