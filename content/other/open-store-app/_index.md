This section contains all legal documentation and privacy policies for **Open Store: TV Apps & Launcher**, a lightweight, privacy-first companion and app hub crafted specifically for Android TV and Google TV devices by Dhruv Patel ([@DearDhruv](https://github.com/DearDhruv)).

Open Store is engineered with an uncompromising **local-first and privacy-by-design architecture**. It provides an instant 1-click gateway to launch the official Google Play Store on TV devices, an installed TV apps drawer with real-time D-pad remote search, quick launch pinned favorites, live storage health monitoring, and quick living-room system settings shortcuts with strictly zero advertisements, zero background analytics telemetry, and zero tracking SDKs.
