**Commit:** `c227a6b`

## 1. Visual & Layout Baseline Findings

### 1.1 Home (`/`)
- **Structure:** Currently mounts `layouts/index.html`, which pulls the entire raw body of `content/about.md` through a regex `replaceRE "<img[^>]*>" ""` filter and appends four plain button links: View Portfolio, Read Blog, Browse Projects, Resume (PDF).
- **Issue:** Homepage duplicates the entire About page rather than functioning as an executive engineering overview with high-impact hero, capability strip, selected work cards, writing highlights, and clear collaboration action.
- **Visuals:** Dark charcoal/ink theme (`#252627`), circular avatar border (`#90a4ae`), terminal-style title text (`$ cd /home/`).

### 1.2 About (`/about/`)
- **Structure:** Uses `layouts/_default/hero-page.html`, rendering the hero avatar + title + subtitle and the Markdown body of `about.md`.
- **Aliases:** Supported via Hugo front matter: `/about-us/`, `/about-hugo/`, `/contact/`.
- **Hierarchy:** Long text lists with emojis. Needs clean scannable sections: Executive Summary, Architecture & Systems Specialization, Career Milestones, Community/Speaking, and Contact.

### 1.3 Portfolio (`/portfolio/`) vs Projects (`/projects/`)
- **Portfolio:** Uses `layouts/_default/hero-page.html` with `content/portfolio.md`. Focuses on executive impact and leadership.
- **Projects:** Uses `layouts/_default/single.html` with `content/projects.md`. Has deep technical bullets for AI Playground and VelociHue.
- **Opportunity:** Distinguish roles clearly: Portfolio = Curated impact overview and categorization (Professional, Personal Products, Open Source, Community); Projects = In-depth case studies with architecture diagrams, constraints, decisions, and performance metrics.

### 1.4 Technical Blog & Series (`/posts/`)
- **Index:** `/posts/` lists articles with dates.
- **Series Landing Page:** `/posts/ai_playground_series/` has markdown links to the 6 parts.
- **Articles:** 6 long-form technical articles with high-quality engineering details (Speculative Turbo decoding, document engine, NDK crash isolation, Bonsai diffusion, mobile hardware limits).
- **Reading Experience:** Typography can be substantially enhanced with readable measure (65–80ch), polished syntax highlighting, copy-code button, responsive Mermaid diagrams, and sticky/accessible series navigation.

### 1.5 Legal & Compliance Pages (`/other/...`)
- 6 legal documents: Terms and Privacy Policies for AI Playground, Open Store App, and VelociHue.
- Formatted as clean markdown legal texts. Must maintain identical routes, copy, and layout clarity.

### 1.6 Sitemap (`/sitemap/` & `/sitemap.xml`)
- `/sitemap/`: Human-readable hierarchical tree with folder icons and badges.
- `/sitemap.xml`: Standard XML sitemap generated by Hugo.
